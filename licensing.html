Enterprise Intelligence Built for Sovereign Control

CAIRN ETP is built for operational independence: entitlement is verified entirely offline, there is no usage telemetry and no remote kill-switch, and the governance layer is deterministic in every edition. If you configure a cloud model provider, that provider receives what you route to it — CAIRN states which of its three privacy states is in force rather than implying nothing ever leaves.

The CAIRN Trust Guarantee: Safety Is Never An Upsell. Default-deny security gates, durable SHA-256 decision ledgers, CRUCIBLE isolated sandboxes (Docker & Windows Sandbox VM), and COMPASS audit provenance ship across every edition. Commercial tiers scale signed corporate policy distribution, independent evidence bundle export, inbound MCP trust gateway, and priority support.
