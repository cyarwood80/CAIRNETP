# ADR-0008: Website Brand, Category Positioning, and Content Governance

* **Status:** Accepted
* **Date:** 2026-07-29
* **Authors:** Chris Yarwood (@cyarwood80) & CAIRN Enterprise Architecture Team
* **Deciders:** Chief Executive Officer, Chief Technology Officer, Chief Architecture Officer

## Context and Problem Statement

As CAIRN ETP evolves into the enterprise market, early digital properties face the risk of diluting brand positioning by mimicking consumer AI startup tropes (glowing neon gradients, hype buzzwords, particle animations, and bloated multi-page software marketing). Enterprise decision-makers (CTOs, CISOs, Chief Risk Officers) looking at £10M+ procurements evaluate vendor trust through gravitas, restraint, clarity, and category-defining vision—not flashy feature lists.

How do we govern future website additions, design aesthetics, copy style, category vocabulary, and content expansions to ensure CAIRN ETP remains the unquestioned category leader for Enterprise Trust?

## Decision Drivers

1. **Word Count & Copy Minimalist Discipline**: Enterprise websites improve when text is reduced by 30–40%. Every paragraph must pass the single-sentence compression test.
2. **Category Vocabulary Ownership**: Consistently reinforce the category terms *Enterprise Trust Platform*, *Trust Plane*, *Evidence-Oriented Reasoning*, *Decision Lineage*, *Audit Provenance*, and *Knowledge Fabric*.
3. **Quality over Quantity (Page Governance)**: Resist adding unnecessary web pages. Maintain a compact set of high-conviction pages answering explicit enterprise buyer questions.
4. **Palantir-Class Aesthetics**: Slate (`#3B4450`), Graphite (`#23272E`), Limestone (`#F5F5F1`), and single signature Oxide Green (`#5B6F5A`). Zero neon, zero purple blobs, zero stock photos of people smiling at laptops.
5. **The Uniqueness Test**: "If the company name were removed from every page, could this website belong to anyone else?" The answer must strictly be *No*.

## Considered Options

* **Option 1: Standard Consumer AI Marketing Site** — High-energy neon animations, buzzwords ("revolutionary", "cutting-edge"), 40+ average pages, feature lists. (Rejected)
* **Option 2: Palantir-Class Trust Plane Governance Model (ADR-0008)** — Problem-first outcome positioning ("We help organisations trust AI"), minimalist authoritative copy, compact high-conviction pages, custom architectural illustrations, and evidence-first proof mechanics. (Accepted)

## Decision Outcome

Chosen Option: **Option 2 (ADR-0008)**.

### Key Governance Mandates:

### 1. Ruthless Word Elimination & Copy Discipline
- Never over-explain. Never apologize. Simply state.
- Replace technical software descriptions ("CAIRN is a hybrid multi-model router using LLMs...") with clear outcome statements ("We help organisations trust AI. Every recommendation is supported by evidence.").
- All proposed marketing copy must be audited to remove 30–40% of fluff before publishing.

### 2. Category Vocabulary Discipline
Consistently reinforce and own the following category-defining terms across all digital assets:
- **Trust Plane** / **Trust Layer**
- **Evidence-Oriented Reasoning**
- **Decision Lineage**
- **Audit Provenance**
- **Enterprise Trust Platform**

### 3. Page Addition & Roadmap Governance
- **Maximum Quality, Minimum Surface Area**: Maintain 6–8 exceptional, Gartner-grade pages rather than 40 average marketing pages.
- **Future Content Roadmap**: Over the coming months, expand digital assets thoughtfully with:
  - Technical Whitepapers & Reference Architectures
  - Security Disclosures & Compliance Documentation
  - API Specifications & Integration Guides
  - Product Lifecycle Documentation & Engineering Logs
  - Architecture Decision Record (ADR) Highlights (without exposing proprietary detail)

### 4. Visual Identity & Custom Architecture Diagrams
- Avoid generic stock photography and generic blue/purple AI palettes.
- Use Slate, Graphite, Limestone, and single-accent Oxide Green.
- Invest in iconic, proprietary architectural diagrams (e.g. *The Trust Layer* contrast diagram: `Evidence` → `Knowledge` → `Trust` → `Reasoning` → `Decision` → `Answer`) that define the CAIRN visual brand identity much like the Kubernetes helm wheel or AWS architecture icons.

### 5. The Brand Uniqueness Test
Before publishing any new page, diagram, or asset, apply the strict test:
*"If we remove the CAIRN name, could this page belong to any generic AI startup?"* If yes, reject and rewrite.

## Positive Consequences

* Establishes CAIRN ETP as the unquestioned category creator and leader for Enterprise Trust.
* Resonates immediately with Chief Risk Officers, CISOs, and enterprise procurement boards.
* Maintains high visual and narrative distinction that competitors cannot easily imitate.
