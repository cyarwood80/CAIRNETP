Why publish the failures. The register exists because an enterprise assessment in August 2026 found the gap between what CAIRN claimed and what CAIRN did was wide enough to fail a procurement review. The failure mode is specific and recurring: something is built, described, then partly disabled or superseded, and the description is never revisited. A register that listed only the successes would carry the credibility of full disclosure while being a highlight reel — which is the same defect one level up.
